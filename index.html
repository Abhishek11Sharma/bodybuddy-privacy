Privacy Policy

Body Buddy · Android app · Last updated 19 November 2026

In short: Body Buddy is an offline learning app about the human body. It collects no personal data and sends nothing off your device.

Information we collect

None. Body Buddy has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

Data stored on your device

  • Your learning progress and settings are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Read-aloud (text-to-speech)

Body Buddy can read lessons aloud using the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device. The app only passes lesson text to that engine; it does not record your voice or use the microphone.

Children's privacy

Body Buddy is made with children in mind. It does not collect, use or disclose personal information from anyone, including children, in line with Google Play's Families policy and COPPA.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
